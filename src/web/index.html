Built around real community work

Community work should be easier to shape and safer to review.

Organizers often piece together forms, messages, spreadsheets, and website updates while trying to keep everyone aligned. Taawun brings that preparation into one guided place, so a team can build a clear page, decide who may change it, and understand what will be shared.

Events and registrations

Shape an iftar or community event page with schedules, announcements, signup questions, and volunteer details. Registration controls are for preview and rehearsal in the current beta, not yet a shared visitor database.

Community information

Bring notices, campaign information, volunteer plans, eligibility notes, and your own fields into one organized page instead of scattering them across disconnected documents.

People and decisions

Invite teammates with clear responsibilities, prepare proposals, record votes, and preserve the final decision so the community process is easier to follow.

Cooperative planning

Explore marketplace and ethical-finance processes in a no-money practice space. Teams can rehearse review and approval steps without moving funds or implying a religious ruling.

Cooperation with clear responsibility

Know who can act, what they reviewed, and where the boundary is.

Taawun means cooperation. The platform is being built around participation with visible responsibility—not a pile of shared passwords or an unexplained “publish” button.

Give people the right responsibility

The workspace owner manages access and future sharing. Maintainers can improve a page and prepare new previews. Viewers can inspect without changing the build. Invitations can be revoked and members can be removed.

Know what your team reviewed

Every completed preview includes proof you can check. It shows the private team space, template, and content, plus where and how long the preview may be used. If that proof no longer matches or has expired, Taawun stops showing the preview as verified.

Record decisions without overclaiming

Teams can prepare proposals, record votes, and preserve final decisions. Those records support accountable community process; they are not religious rulings or evidence of scholar approval.

Clear information and money boundaries: Day-to-day community page records are designed to live in community members’ browsers first. Taawun centrally keeps account details, created previews, and the history needed to protect and operate the service. When members share community records between browsers, Taawun may carry them over an encrypted connection, and some required history can remain after account deletion. Taawun does not hold funds or settle payments. Donation, escrow, Zakat, Qard Hasan, stipend, and revenue-sharing flows are no-money practice environments—not transactions, religious determinations, or scholar-approved rulings.

Questions from community organizers

Understand Taawun before you create an account.

What is Taawun?

Taawun is a guided community page builder for mosques, charities, Muslim student associations, and grassroots groups. It combines customizable starting points, clear team responsibilities, decision records, and a private review step.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The guided path lets you choose a template, select page parts, edit ordinary fields, add your own information, and review the result without writing code.

Is Taawun a mosque website builder?

It can prepare and review community and event pages in a private workspace. It is not yet a public replacement for a mosque website because we have not completed a real launch on a community-owned web address.

What can I build today?

You can customize a Community Iftar page, a broader Community Workspace, or a Bazaar Cooperative practice page using eleven building blocks. The result is a private preview your invited teammates can inspect.

Can Taawun collect registrations from our whole community?

You can design and test registration questions and controls. The beta does not yet collect everyone’s answers in one shared place.

How does Taawun protect a preview?

The service gives each completed preview proof you can check. It confirms the exact page, private team space, content, and where and how long the preview may be used. If something is missing, changed, or expired, Taawun does not show the preview as verified.

Where is our information kept?

Community page records are designed to stay in members’ browsers first. Taawun centrally keeps account details, created previews, and the history needed to protect and operate the service. When members share records between browsers, Taawun may carry them over an encrypted connection, and required history may remain after account deletion.

Does Taawun process donations or payments?

No. Finance features are practice workflows for reviewing steps and approvals. Taawun does not hold funds, process escrow, settle payments, or calculate a binding religious obligation.

Is Taawun Shariah-certified or scholar-approved?

No. Ethical and religious references are clearly marked as reference-only. They do not replace a qualified scholar, create a religious ruling, or certify a community’s work.

Can we publish on our own web address?

The platform can prepare and check a web address controlled by your community, but we have not yet completed a real launch there. Public sharing remains a guided beta step reviewed with you, not a self-service promise.
