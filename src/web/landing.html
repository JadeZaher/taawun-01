Cooperation with visible responsibility

Review what was made—not a vague promise about it.

Every completed private preview comes with a receipt that identifies the team space, the chosen parts, the content and the approved places and time in which it may be used. When that proof is missing, changed or expired, Taawun stops describing the preview as verified.

Private beta boundary: Taawun does not hold funds or settle payments. Decision records are not religious rulings or evidence of scholar approval. Public sharing on a community-owned web address still requires external domain control and authorized review.

Questions from organizers

Understand Taawun before creating an account.

What is Taawun?

Taawun is a guided community app builder for mosques, charities, Muslim student groups and grassroots organizers. It combines customizable starting points, clear team responsibilities, decision records and a protected review step.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Choose a template, select the parts you need, edit ordinary fields, add your own information and review the result.

Can it replace our public mosque website today?

Not yet. You can prepare and review community pages privately. A public launch still needs a community-controlled web address and authorized review.

Can it collect registrations or move money?

You can design registration questions and rehearse financial review steps. The current beta does not provide a shared public response database, hold funds or settle payments.

How does Taawun help a team review safely?

A completed preview includes server-checked proof of the exact workspace, content, approved uses and expiry. Team roles limit who may inspect, change or prepare future sharing.
