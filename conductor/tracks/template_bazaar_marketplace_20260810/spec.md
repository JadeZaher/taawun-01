# Spec: The Bazaar — Community Template Directory & 1-Click Customization Engine

**Track ID**: `template_bazaar_marketplace_20260810`  
**Status**: Planned  

---

## 1. Background
Operates as the PaaS economic engine of Taawun, allowing community creators to publish app templates and users to customize and deploy them in 1-click.

---

## 2. Requirements
- **FR-1**: Template Publishing & Vetting pipeline.
- **FR-2**: 1-Click Prompt Customization ("Make the theme green and point data to my city").
- **FR-3**: Free staging preview before transaction (Anti-Gharar enforcement).
- **FR-4**: Flat infrastructure fee billing with revenue sharing for template creators (Riba-free model).
